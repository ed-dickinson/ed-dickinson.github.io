I've been walking round with my pocket full of acorns.
I've been told it brings good luck,
and i believe a good amount of what i hear,
i'd rather trust a falsehood than hold doubt for the truth.

I like to think the mighty oaks will look out for me,
since i hold their beloved children in my care.
The kind of world that's imbued with benevolence,
whether i imagine it, or it actually is.

I carry them around so long they break apart in my hands.
I wonder how something with such a healthy gleam
                     could hold a nut so black and withered?

Now i start to see myself in those tiny seeds,
what potential held within their form.
The love i feel, for these things,
could it be how the world feels when it thinks of me?

I see the untapped life inside and i know it will die
if not planted, just as i will too.
Unless i go and sow myself in the soil of the people,
a community that will nourish me and kindly help me grow.

I hope so much that i can give back what’s been gift to me,
that i can be to these acorns as the world has been to me—

                                      Nourisher & Protector.

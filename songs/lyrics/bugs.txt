Don't believe anyone
  who says that i was born yesterday.
I have danced with the bugs
  over streams of light where they play.

Laid down in the crop,
  smelt the blade turn it to hay.
I have drunk from the frost,
  as the morning whittles it’s shape.

O, i have lived, if only just for a day!

All my wildest dreams have up and left,
  they wrestled me and ran away.
Left me here with the tame ones,
  that didn’t have the guts to grow beyond today.

I’m not scared to bleed,
                    because what i love
  can smell the blood of a christian man.
So smear it all over me,
                    cover my skin,
  draw out the beast that lies within me.

O, i have lived, if only just for a day!

I remember not being able to understand
  why anyone would die by their own hand.
Sometimes i’d do anything to go back
  to that innocence of heart
          that’s never known what it’s lost.
But i’m born again
  when the morning light breaks through the dark.
I’m born again,
        to live this day like it’s my first.

O, i have lived, if only just for a day!

I am not a bad man,
    though i have said bad things.
I aim to be
    honest from now on.

And though sometimes it's hard
    to align your words with your heart,

Strawberries taste sweeter on the tongue
        when they've been kissed by the sun.

If i only knew,
    the real fruit was you.

And though i have a good name,
    it’s only good for as long as i don’t
    say something that hurts.

You’ll be my biggest believer until you’ve seen
    how i get in the height of the season.

I miss you so much
it feels like i've lost my heart
the only way i know i haven’t
is much i still feel for you.

And all i do is think about you——
all i am is someone who isn’t there with you.

My heart is gone, and so i am gone——
but at least i’m someone
who remembers being there with you.

And i cannot close my eyes
for a moment without thinking of you
as if you’re really there——
like i can hear you just in the next room.

I would give up everything to have you
turn around annnnnnd come into this room——
with me on your mind.

With that look i’ve been imagining,
and make me someone——
someone who is really there with you.

Mountain of Happiness, i know your name,
                  but i don’t know your shape,
 the shape of your silhouette as i walked down
           from the last great day of my life.

If i would have known that that was it then,
                      i would never have left.
      If i could have stayed under those peaks
                             with you i would.
I would have bathed
    in the glow of that pinkening sky forever.

                                      Forever.

I will look back at that moment
                      for the rest of my life.
A statue in time,
           a testament to true happiness found
                       in the lingering warmth
               of a softly dying summer night.

To be among a friend or a lover
  and to think of nothing but being with them.

—and to know that there are mountains
                 like this all over the world.

                        Mountain of Happiness!

I thought i would do anything for you,
but it turns out i won’t do much at all.

My soul has gone and i’m losing the will
to live without you,
when all i had to do was tell someone about it.

I knew you’d stay here anyway
so i just sat back and didn't.
I knew i should do more for you, not shying away!


I would confront my oldest friend—— myself
and ask, why have we grown apart?

It used to be so clear to us
how much we loved this life.
Now why is it so hard to find something
that sparks the simple joy
of just being alive on earth with you?

It's the kind of thing worth doing for...
doing anything for.


I will consult the quiet things
that talk to me when i am alone.

They say if i really would do anything
it starts with letting you go,
but i rarely these days feel as good
as when i pretend i'm still with you.

It's hard to think of anything as good!
But i must because it's the only thing
                        that's truly left for me.

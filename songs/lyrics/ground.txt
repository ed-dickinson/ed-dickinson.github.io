A blue sky holds promise for me,
just like you once did,
with dreams shaped like clouds,
big as sheep wandering the fields of the sky,
or gentle cows raining milk into my open mouth.

I have seen how goddamn sweet
my life looked alongside you,
when all i needed was the ground at my feet
                      and your eyes fixed on me.

I don’t know where on earth i would be,
                          if i had not of met ye
or had the treasures you’ve left me with.
Such golden things i could never give up,
because i’m so much happier now,
                 as the person you helped me be.

I have seen how goddamn sweet
my life was alongside you,
when all i had was the ground at my feet
                      and your arms around me.

There’s some things i would never give up
even to be back there alongside you.
Even now after all of these years
i still treasure the person you made me.

I can feel my elbows cracking,
    as i raise my two hands up
            into the grip of the sky
    that bends to take me,
               and ensures that i grow——

when i find it hard to be thankful
and harder than i should to have hope——
      when i find it hard to be grateful
 for the grace i have been touched with.

I can feel my lip skin cracking
    where my mouth has been pried open
                            in total awe
of the force that runs right through me,
        and ensures that i grow——

when i find it hard to be thankful,
& harder than i should to stay grateful.

When i find it hard to be thankful
and to stay grateful.

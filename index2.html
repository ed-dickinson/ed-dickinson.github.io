Songs of Myself


An ode that tells of my great love for the world. An album that will change and grow with me as the seasons and years progress, as I add new songs, tinker with old ones, and prune the ones that lose their luster.

I wanted a home for the songs I write, somewhere they fit, wherever they came from in my life. So I thought I could have one long album that I add to as I go, representing my life through the songs. I was inspired by my endless, majestic hero Walt Whitman, who kept adding to his poetic opus ‘Leaves of Grass’ for as long as he lived.
